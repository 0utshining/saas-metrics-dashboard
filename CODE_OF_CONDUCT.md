# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the SaaS Metrics Dashboard community a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We are committed to creating an environment where people can collaborate regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful and constructive
- Giving and accepting useful technical feedback
- Focusing discussions on the project and its goals
- Showing empathy toward other contributors
- Respecting differing viewpoints and experiences
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, or discriminatory comments
- Personal attacks, insults, or hostile conduct
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behavior
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Sexualized language or unwanted sexual attention
- Conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and the project.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces, including GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project-related communication.

It also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in a public setting.

## Reporting

If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the GitHub profile and request a private communication channel.

Do not use a public issue to report sensitive interpersonal concerns.

Reports will be reviewed fairly and with appropriate discretion. Information will be shared only as necessary to address the situation.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may use the following responses depending on context and severity:

1. **Correction** — A private or public request to change inappropriate behavior.
2. **Warning** — A formal warning with expectations for future conduct.
3. **Temporary restriction** — Temporary limits on participation.
4. **Permanent restriction** — Permanent removal from project participation.
