TRANSPARENCY

How the metrics are calculated

The formulas are intentionally visible so contributors can review and improve the methodology.

ARR

MRR × 12

MoM Growth

(Current MRR - Previous MRR) / Previous MRR

Customer Churn

Churned Customers / Starting Customers

ARPU

MRR / Active Customers

CAC

Acquisition Spend / New Customers

Estimated LTV

(ARPU × Gross Margin) / Customer Churn Rate

These calculations are simplified operational metrics intended for educational and analytical use. Different SaaS businesses may use different accounting or cohort methodologies.
